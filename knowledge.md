## Buying $LIGHT in the presale — the five steps

Anyone asking "how do I buy" gets this shape. `/howtobuy` posts the full card;
point them there if they want it step by step.

**1. Get BNB on an exchange.**
Any mainstream centralised exchange that lists BNB works — Binance, Coinbase,
Kraken, MEXC and others. They need an account, ID verification, and a card or
bank transfer. This part takes the longest, often a day or two for a first
verification, so tell people to start here rather than leaving it to the last
hour of the sale.

**2. Make a self-custody wallet.**
MetaMask or Trust Wallet, both free. During setup it shows a **recovery
phrase** (12 or 24 words). Written on paper, kept offline, never typed into
anything, never photographed, never sent to anyone including me and including
the founder. Anyone who asks for it is stealing from them, no exceptions.
The wallet must be set to the **BNB Smart Chain** network.

**3. Send the BNB from the exchange to that wallet.**
This is the step that loses people money, so slow down here:
- On the withdrawal screen the network must say **BNB Smart Chain**, also
  written **BEP20**. Not BEP2, not Beacon Chain, not Ethereum/ERC-20.
- Not every exchange offers BEP20 withdrawals for BNB, and it changes. If the
  network picker doesn't offer BNB Smart Chain, they should use an exchange
  that does rather than picking the closest-looking option. Sending on the
  wrong network usually means the funds are gone.
- Copy-paste the wallet address. Never retype it.
- Send a small test amount first if they're nervous. It costs pennies in fees
  and it's the difference between losing $5 and losing everything.
- Leave a little BNB spare for gas — roughly 0.002 BNB is plenty. A wallet
  with exactly the contribution amount and nothing left over cannot pay the
  fee to contribute.

**4. Contribute on PinkSale.**
Open the official presale link (it's in /ca and the pin), connect the wallet,
enter an amount, confirm in the wallet popup. Minimum is 0.05 BNB, maximum is
1 BNB per wallet.

**5. Claim after it ends.**
Tokens are not in the wallet straight away. Once the sale closes and is
finalised, go back to the same PinkSale page and claim. Same wallet that
contributed, same page — there is no separate claim site, and anyone linking
one is running a scam.

## Words people will trip over

Short and plain. Nobody wants a lecture.

- **Wallet** — an app holding the keys to funds on a blockchain. Self-custody
  means nobody else can freeze or move them, and nobody else can recover them
  either.
- **Recovery / seed phrase** — the 12 or 24 words that ARE the wallet. Whoever
  has them has the money.
- **Gas** — the network fee for a transaction, paid in BNB. A few cents on BNB
  Smart Chain.
- **BEP-20** — the token standard on BNB Smart Chain. $LIGHT is one.
- **Presale** — buying before public listing, at a fixed rate, through PinkSale
  which holds the funds under contract rules until the sale resolves.
- **Liquidity / LP** — the pool that lets people trade after listing. Locking it
  means the team can't pull it.
- **Slippage** — the gap between quoted and filled price when trading. Relevant
  after listing, not during the presale.
- **Burn** — sending tokens to an address nobody controls, removing them from
  supply permanently.
